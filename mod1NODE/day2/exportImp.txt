Golden Rule
require() always returns:
whatever module.exports contains

Repeat:

require()
returns
module.exports

Entire CommonJS revolves around this.
___________X_______________X_______________


Initially:
exports === module.exports

Both point to same object.
Example:
exports.add = add;
exports.sub = sub;

Equivalent to:
module.exports.add = add;
module.exports.sub = sub;
